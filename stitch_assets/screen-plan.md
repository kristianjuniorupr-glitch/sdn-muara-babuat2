# Project: Website Resmi SD Negeri Muara Babuat 2
## Screen List Proposal

1. **Beranda (Home)**: High-impact hero, Visi & Misi, Kepala Sekolah introduction, and Ekstrakurikuler preview.
2. **Profil Sekolah (School Profile)**: Comprehensive page featuring Sejarah Sekolah, Data Guru (PNS, PPPK, Kontrak), and Data Peserta Didik (Class 1-6 statistics).
3. **Pengumuman PPDB (Enrollment News)**: A dedicated portal for potential students with an announcement list.
4. **Detail Pengumuman (Announcement Detail)**: A clean reading view for specific PPDB announcements and documents.

## Design Direction
The design will prioritize a 'National Institutional' aesthetic—clean, structured, and modern. We will use real school terminology and maintain high contrast for accessibility.
